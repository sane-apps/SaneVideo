SaneVideo Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 17, 2026.

Local media

SaneVideo is designed for local video work. Recording, imported media, editing projects, captions, demo packs, and exports remain on your Mac unless you deliberately move or upload files yourself.

Permissions

The app requests camera, microphone, and screen recording permissions only for recording features. macOS controls those permissions and you can revoke them in System Settings.

Network use

SaneVideo may contact SaneApps services for app updates, license or App Store purchase validation, aggregate app counts, and support diagnostics you choose to send. Direct YouTube upload is not included in this build.

Support

Support email may include information you choose to provide, including diagnostics if you copy them from the app. Contact hi@saneapps.com.
