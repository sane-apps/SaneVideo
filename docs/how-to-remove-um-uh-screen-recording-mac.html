How to Cut Um and Uh From a Screen Recording on Mac

Filler sounds and long pauses make a screen recording drag. SaneVideo finds them and cuts them from the timeline in one click, on your Mac.

Updated 12 November 2026

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.
  • SaneVideo, free from this site or the Mac App Store.
  • An internet connection the first time. Clean Up downloads its speech model, Parakeet, once. It is about 600 MB. After that it works offline.

Steps

  1. Record with SaneVideo, or choose File > Import Video... to open a recording you already have.
  2. Check the speech engine. Open Settings, then Privacy & AI. Under Captions & Transcripts, set Speech engine to Parakeet. It is the default. Apple's built-in engine leaves "um" and "uh" out of its transcript, so Remove Fillers would find nothing to cut.
  3. In Edit, select your clip. The Clean Up panel is open on the right.
  4. Turn on Remove Fillers. Turn on Remove Silence too if you want the pauses gone. Its Quieter than slider sets how quiet counts as silent, from -60 dB to -20 dB. It starts at -45 dB; move it right to cut more.
  5. Press Clean Up, or Shift-Command-M. SaneVideo transcribes the clip, finds the filler sounds and quiet stretches, and cuts them from the timeline.
  6. Play it back. If it cut too much, press Command-Z, change the settings and press Clean Up again.
  7. Press Command-E to export. If Generate Captions was on, your export comes with a matching .srt caption file.

The Presets menu has two shortcuts: Quick Fix only removes silence, and Full Cleanup turns on all five options.

What counts as a filler

Clean Up cuts without asking first, so it only cuts sounds that are never real words: um, uh, er, ah and hmm, longer spellings such as umm and uhh, and mhm, uh-huh and mm-hmm. Words that often carry meaning, such as "like", "so", "well", "actually" and "you know", stay in.

What it can't do

  • It doesn't catch stutters or repeated words. Cut those by hand: put the playhead on the spot, press Command-B to split, and delete the piece.
  • It doesn't show you a list of cuts to approve first. Play the result back, and undo if it went too far.
  • With the Apple (built in) speech engine, it can still cut silences but not filler sounds.

Questions

Does SaneVideo upload my video to find the ums?

No. Clean Up runs on your Mac. The only download is the speech model, once, the first time you use Clean Up or captions.

Can I undo Clean Up?

Yes. Command-Z undoes the whole Clean Up. You can also change the settings and press Run Again With These Settings.

Does it cut words like "like" and "you know"?

No. Clean Up only cuts sounds that are never real words, such as um, uh, er and hmm. Words that often carry meaning stay in.
