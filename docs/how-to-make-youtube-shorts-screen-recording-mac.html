How to Make YouTube Shorts From a Screen Recording on Mac

You already have a long screen recording. SaneVideo can find strong moments in it and export them as vertical Shorts, on your Mac, in a few clicks.

Updated 12 November 2026

What you need

  • A Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.
  • SaneVideo, free from this site or the Mac App Store.
  • A recording longer than the Short you want. A 60-second Short needs more than 60 seconds of video.

Steps

  1. Record or import your video. Run Clean Up first if you want the ums and pauses gone: Shorts leave out what Clean Up cut and what you trimmed off the clip.
  2. Open Create Shorts. Choose Shorts... from the Export menu in the toolbar, or File > Create Shorts... (Shift-Command-R). SaneVideo uses the longest clip in your project.
  3. Under Platform, choose YouTube Shorts. That sets Duration to 60s and Aspect Ratio to 9:16.
  4. Set Max Shorts, from 1 to 10. It starts at 5.
  5. Press Analyze. SaneVideo listens for pauses and stand-out sounds such as laughter, and looks for the part of the frame that draws the eye. It lists up to Max Shorts clips that don't overlap, in the order they appear, all selected. Click a clip to leave it out.
  6. Press the export button. It counts your picks, such as Export 5 Shorts. SaneVideo saves each Short as an MP4, 1080 x 1920, in Movies/SaneVideo/Shorts and shows it in Finder.
  7. Upload the files in YouTube Studio or the YouTube app. SaneVideo doesn't upload for you.

What it can't do

  • You can't move the crop. A wide screen recording cropped to 9:16 keeps a tall strip about a third of the width, centered where the eye goes. Small text near the edges will be cut off.
  • Shorts are cut from the original recording. Clean Up cuts and trims carry over; framing, background and color effects don't, and captions aren't burned in.
  • Each Short is one stretch of your video at the length you chose. You can't join moments from different parts into one Short.

Questions

How long can a Short from SaneVideo be?

You can choose 15, 30, 60 or 90 seconds. The YouTube Shorts choice picks 60. If Clean Up cut parts of that stretch, the Short runs a little shorter.

Can I make square or 4:5 clips too?

Yes. Aspect Ratio offers 9:16 (1080 x 1920), 1:1 (1080 x 1080) and 4:5 (1080 x 1350).

Where do my Shorts go?

Into Movies/SaneVideo/Shorts on your Mac, named after your project, such as "My Tutorial Short 1".
