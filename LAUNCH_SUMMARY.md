## 🎯 Key Differentiators (Marketing Focus)

### 1. Privacy-First AI
- **Message**: "100% on-device processing. Your videos never leave your Mac."
- **Competitive Advantage**: Descript/Loom require cloud processing
- **Target**: Privacy-conscious creators, enterprises, healthcare

### 2. Unified Workflow
- **Message**: "Record + Edit in One App. No more switching between tools."
- **Competitive Advantage**: Only app that combines both natively

- **Competitive Advantage**: Camtasia/Descript are subscription-only
- **Target**: Users tired of recurring fees

### 4. Apple Silicon Optimized

- **Competitive Advantage**: Maximum performance, thermal-aware rendering
