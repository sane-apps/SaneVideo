Built for footage you can't put in the cloud.

Loom, Descript, and most recorders upload every clip to their servers to store, process, and transcribe it. SaneVideo doesn't. Record product demos under NDA, internal training, support sessions with customer data, and unreleased features — and keep every frame on your Mac, where compliance and common sense say it belongs.

If your footage can show customer data, internal systems, unreleased work, or anything under contract, it shouldn't pass through a vendor's servers to get recorded.
