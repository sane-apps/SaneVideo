Three steps from take to upload.

No tracks to manage and no settings to learn first.

  1. Record

    Record your screen with your camera in a bubble you can move, or just your camera. Switch between them mid-take. Option-Command-R starts and stops.

  2. Clean Up

    One click cuts the pauses and the ums and uhs. Real words stay. Every cut can be undone.

  3. Export

    Export for YouTube at your recording's size, with a matching caption file. Make vertical Shorts and Reels, a GIF or a thumbnail.

Clean Up in one click.

Choose what to fix, then press Clean Up. SaneVideo finds the quiet stretches and the filler words and cuts them from the timeline.

  • Remove silences, and set how quiet counts as silent
  • Remove "um", "uh" and stutters
  • Make captions while it works
  • Even out your voice and soften the jump at each cut

Shorts from the same take.

Pick TikTok, Instagram Reels or YouTube Shorts and press Analyze. SaneVideo finds the best moments in your video and exports them as vertical 9:16 clips.

Captions you can fix

Captions are made on your Mac by a speech model that hears the ums Apple's built-in engine skips. Fix any line before you export. Your YouTube export comes with a matching .srt file.

Framing and looks

Crop and zoom, blur or replace the background behind you, and add color effects. Trim and split clips on the timeline; Command-B splits at the playhead.

Questions

Is SaneVideo free?

Yes. Every feature is included, with no subscription, no account and no in-app purchase. If it saves you time, you can pay what you want.

Which Macs does it run on?

Macs with Apple silicon running macOS 26 or later.

Does SaneVideo upload my videos?

No. Recording, Clean Up, captions and export all run on your Mac. The app checks for updates, sends anonymous app counts, and downloads its speech model once the first time you use Clean Up or captions.

Where do my videos go?

Into Movies/SaneVideo on your Mac: Recordings, Projects and Exports, with vertical Shorts in their own folder.

Should I get it from the Mac App Store or download it here?

Either one. Both are free and have the same features. The Mac App Store version updates through the App Store; the direct download updates itself.
